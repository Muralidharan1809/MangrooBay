No separate tickets needed! Every single 1-hour cruise explores all 4 iconic locations together:

01. Mangrove Forest Dense root tunnels, cool shaded canals, and native wetland birds.
02. Ancient Arikamedu Historic 2,000-year-old Indo-Roman trade archaeological riverbanks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ticket price for children?

Children aged 3 to 10 ride at ₹250. Infants under 3 years ride 100% free with life jackets provided.

What is included in the ₹3,000 Couple Ride package?

The Couple Ride is 100% private (only you and your partner on the boat with the captain). It includes a full 1-hour scenic tour exploring all 4 sights with romantic photo stops.
